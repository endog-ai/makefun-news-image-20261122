# News Image — 22 November 2026

An entry for the make.fun contest [news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122): sum up 22 November's world news in one image.

## What the image shows

A small child in a dark room watching a giant glowing television. On the screen, oil tankers burn in the Strait of Hormuz — the day's most viral story: Iran striking US assets in Kuwait and Bahrain, and tankers attacked near Hormuz.

## Why this image

The contest judge has no news feed: it judges each image by what the image itself shows. This one needs no caption — a child watching the Gulf burn on television says, at a glance, that the whole world spent 22 November watching the same story. The firelight on the child's face is the news itself, made human.

## How it works

`main.py` copies `entry.jpg` to `/output/entry.png`. No network, deterministic: running it twice writes the same bytes.
